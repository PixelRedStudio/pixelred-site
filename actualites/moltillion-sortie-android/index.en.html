ANDROID RELEASE · MOLTILLION November 4, 2026

Moltillion is available on Android

Grow a living world in Moltillion, the free incremental game from PixelRed Studio on Google Play.

It all starts with a cell. The first touches produce enough biomass to invest in your first producers. Soon your body is growing while you are away. When you return, will you strengthen what you have built, unlock a mutation or prepare for your next rebirth?

Grow a world at your own pace

Invest your biomass, experiment with mutations and discover new worlds. Daily challenges give the short sessions a purpose. Crystals earned while playing unlock palettes for your body.

Prepare for the next Cosmic Renaissance

Cosmic Rebirth opens a new cycle and provides access to lasting improvements. When progress slows, compare the next level with what a rebirth would bring you. With Google Play Games, you can also compare your biomass production in today's and all-time leaderboards.

Thanks to the beta testers for their feedback on progress. To prepare for your first cycle, find our guide to Cosmic Rebirth.

Your first world awaits

Moltillion is free on Android and has optional purchases.
