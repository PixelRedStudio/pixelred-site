ANDROID RELEASE · BOULITO November 8, 2026

Boulito is available on Android

Boulito, the arena survival roguelite from PixelRed Studio, is available on Google Play. Assemble your weapons and survive the twenty waves.

A factory out of control, a robot and twenty waves of broken machines. In Boulito, every break is an opportunity to rethink your arsenal before the arena doors open again.

Six weapons, multiple ways to survive

Equip up to six weapons, combine duplicates to strengthen them, and choose your upgrades to suit the way you play. Collect the Voltins before the end of the wave to finance your next purchases. If you survive the twentieth, endless mode awaits you.

From beta to Google Play

The Android version features new visuals for weapons and stats, a preview of fusions, and a reminder when collecting is nearing its end. Thanks to those who tested the game and shared their feedback.

To prepare for your first game, read our survival guide or watch it official trailer. The Windows edition and its free demo are also in the studio shop.

Enter the arena

Boulito is free on Android and offers optional purchases.
