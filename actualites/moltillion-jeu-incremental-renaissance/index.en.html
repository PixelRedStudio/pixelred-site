Incremental game: when to do a rebirth?

In an incremental game, starting over can be the best way to move forward. Here's how to read prestige timing and prepare for a more efficient cycle in Moltillion.

An incremental game transforms small actions into increasingly rapid progress. Then comes a question: should we continue to produce resources in the current cycle or trigger a renaissance to strengthen the following ones? In Moltillion, the Cosmic Rebirth restarts part of the progression and provides access to prestige genes useful for future cycles.

Understanding what the reset exchanges

A revival is not a free reward: it exchanges part of your immediate progress for a long-term advantage. Before enabling it, clearly check what the game keeps and what it resets. Next, compare the prestige bonus offered with the time it would take you to obtain a significant improvement by continuing your cycle.

If the next step is near and your production is still accelerating, waiting may be worthwhile. If each improvement takes much longer and the revival gives a lasting gain, starting again can make the next cycle faster. The right choice depends on the pace of your game, not a universal threshold.

Prepare the next cycle

Before starting again, complete the accessible objectives which may disappear with the reset. Also look at the upgrades available with prestige genes: some can support general production, others encourage a different way of evolving. Read their effect before spending your points, then choose a priority rather than scattering your resources.

  • Check what will be kept and reset.
  • Complete cycle objectives that are almost complete.
  • Compare the next major cost to the advertised revival bonus.
  • Choose a permanent improvement consistent with your next goal.

Do not neglect previous levels

In an incremental, the most recent level is not always the only source of progression. The first levels can produce resources or unlock effects that speed up the whole thing. If an old improvement costs little compared to your current production, it can be profitable. Check the effects displayed and compare the gains rather than going up each level by reflex.

Give each run a goal

Progression becomes more readable when a cycle has a specific goal: unlock an evolution, complete a daily challenge or test a prestige improvement. Moltillion offers mutations and daily challenges to vary the objectives between rebirths. A secondary objective can make a cycle interesting even if the main meter advances slowly.

A personal rhythm rather than a fixed rule

Some players prefer to keep sessions short and respawn often; others like to take it one cycle further. Compare your gains over a few runs and note how long each strategy takes you. You will then see if waiting a long time really pays off more, or if several short cycles accelerate your account better.

Useful reference: trigger revival when the value of its lasting bonus exceeds what you can reasonably obtain by continuing your current cycle, after completing objectives that would be lost.
